Size Guide & Fit Tips

How to measure

  • Chest / bust: measure around the fullest part, keeping the tape horizontal.
  • Waist: measure around your natural waistline, just above the belly button.
  • Length: measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the hem.

Fit names

Slim Fit sits close to the body; size up if you prefer comfort. Regular Fit is our standard cut. Relaxed / Oversized Fit is roomy; size down for a regular look.

Fabric and shrinkage

Our 100% cotton T-shirts are pre-shrunk, but may shrink up to 3% after the first wash in hot water. Wash in cold water and dry in shade to keep the fit.
