Garment Care

  • Cotton T-shirts and shirts: machine wash cold, inside out, do not tumble dry.
  • Denim: wash rarely, in cold water, inside out, to keep the colour.
  • Kurtas with embroidery: hand wash or gentle cycle, dry in shade, iron on the reverse side.
  • Hoodies and jackets: wash cold, do not iron prints.

Sustainability

Our Basics range uses 100% organic cotton sourced from farms in Madhya Pradesh. Our packaging is plastic-free and made from recycled paper.

Recycle programme: send back any 3 old UrbanThread garments with your next return pickup and receive a Rs 200 voucher (minimum order Rs 1,499).
