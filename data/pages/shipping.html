Shipping & Delivery

UrbanThread ships from our warehouse in Bengaluru using three courier partners: BlueDart, Delhivery and Ekart. The partner is chosen automatically based on your PIN code.

Dispatch times

In-stock orders placed before 2 PM IST are dispatched the same day (Monday to Saturday). Orders placed on Sundays and public holidays are dispatched the next working day.

Festive season (Diwali sale)

During the Diwali sale (20 October to 5 November 2026) please allow 2 extra business days for delivery because of high order volumes.

Tracking

Tracking links are sent by SMS and e-mail once the order is shipped. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first update.

Failed delivery

The courier attempts delivery up to 3 times. If all attempts fail, the parcel returns to our warehouse and prepaid orders are refunded automatically (minus shipping charges).
