## 1. About UrbanThread

UrbanThread is an online clothing store based in Bengaluru, India. We sell casual wear for men
and women: T-shirts, shirts, jeans, kurtas, hoodies and jackets. All prices are in Indian
Rupees (INR, Rs) and include GST.

## 2. Orders

**How do I track my order?**
After your order is shipped you receive an SMS and e-mail with a tracking link. You can also
ask ShopAssist (our chat assistant) with your order ID (format `UT1001`) or the phone number
used at checkout.

**Can I change or cancel my order?**
You can cancel an order free of charge until it is shipped (status "placed" or "packed").
Once an order is shipped it cannot be cancelled; please place a return after delivery instead.

**What do the order statuses mean?**
- placed – we received your order and payment (or COD confirmation)
- packed – your items are packed at our warehouse
- shipped – handed over to the courier
- out_for_delivery – the courier will deliver today
- delivered – delivered to your address
- delayed – the courier has reported a delay; we will SMS you a new date
- returned – the return was received at our warehouse
- cancelled – the order was cancelled

## 3. Delivery

- We deliver to all serviceable PIN codes in India through our courier partners.
- **Metro cities** (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune): 2–4 business days.
- **Rest of India**: 4–7 business days. North-East, J&K and island PIN codes: up to 10 business days.
- **Shipping charges**: free on orders of Rs 999 and above. Orders below Rs 999 pay a flat Rs 79.
- **Express delivery** (metro cities only): Rs 149 extra, delivered in 1–2 business days.
- We do not ship outside India at the moment.
- Orders placed after 2 PM IST are processed the next business day.

## 4. Payment & Cash on Delivery (COD)

- We accept UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking, popular wallets and Cash on Delivery.
- **COD** is available for orders between Rs 299 and Rs 10,000.
- A COD handling fee of Rs 49 applies per order.
- COD is not available for express delivery or for customers with two or more refused COD orders.
- Please keep exact change ready; the courier may also accept UPI at the door.

## 5. Returns & Exchanges

- **Return window: 15 days from the date of delivery.**
- Items must be unused, unwashed and with original tags and packaging.
- **Not returnable**: innerwear, socks, face masks, items bought on "Final Sale", and customised items.
- Start a return from "My Orders" on the website or by asking ShopAssist with your order ID.
- We arrange a free reverse pickup in most PIN codes. If pickup is not available, you can
  self-ship the item and we reimburse up to Rs 100 of courier cost.
- **Exchanges** for a different size are free once per order, subject to stock.

## 6. Refunds

- Refunds are processed after the returned item passes a quality check at our warehouse
  (usually within 2 business days of receiving it).
- **Prepaid orders**: refunded to the original payment method in 5–7 business days.
- **COD orders**: refunded to a bank account / UPI ID you provide, in 5–7 business days.
- Shipping and COD fees are non-refundable, except when the item was damaged or wrong.
- **Damaged or wrong item**: report within 48 hours of delivery with photos; we offer a free
  replacement or a full refund including shipping.
- **Refunds above Rs 5,000 need manual approval by a senior support agent.** The chat
  assistant cannot approve them and will create a support ticket instead.

Our "Relaxed Fit" range runs about one size larger; our "Slim Fit" range runs about half a size smaller.

## 8. Contacting a human

If ShopAssist cannot solve your problem, it will create a support ticket. A human agent will
contact you by SMS or phone within 4 business hours. Urgent issues (damaged items, payment
deducted twice, refunds above Rs 5,000) are marked high priority.
